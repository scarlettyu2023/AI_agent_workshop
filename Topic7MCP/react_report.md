# ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models — Citation Network Report

## Summary
The seed paper "ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models" (2022) by Shunyu Yao, Jeffrey Zhao, Dian Yu et al. addresses the separation of reasoning and acting in large language models (LLMs). The authors explore how these models can improve decision-making processes by integrating chain-of-thought prompting for reasoning and action plan generation. Their significant contribution lies in the proposal of a holistic approach that enhances the capabilities of LLMs across various tasks in language understanding and interactive decision-making.
